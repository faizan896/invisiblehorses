01

A key for each agent.

Choose read or payment access. Revoke a key when the work is done. Agent keys cannot approve their own requests.

02

A budget before a payment.

Set company and agent limits, daily budgets and allowed vendors. Pending requests reserve their share of the budget.

03

A trail you can follow.
